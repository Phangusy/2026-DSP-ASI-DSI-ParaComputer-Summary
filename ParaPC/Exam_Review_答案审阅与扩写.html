Q1云计算与交付模型 · Cloud delivery and service models

STEMQuestion stem（原题）：InGen is developing a dinosaur genome-analysis system. Most of the year, the system is used by only a few researchers. However, for six weeks each year, a large genome-reconstruction campaign requires hundreds of processors. InGen already owns a small private data centre containing confidential genetic data. Management proposes three designs: Design A — buy enough servers for the maximum workload and keep them in the InGen data centre all year. Design B — keep confidential data and normal workloads on InGen's infrastructure, but temporarily rent additional public-cloud resources during the annual campaign. Design C — move everything to a cloud provider but reserve a fixed number of machines for the entire year.
题干译文：InGen 正在开发一套恐龙基因组分析系统。一年中大部分时间只有少数研究人员使用；但每年有六周，一次大规模基因组重建任务需要数百个处理器。InGen 已拥有一个小型私有数据中心，存放机密基因数据。管理层提出三种方案：方案 A —— 买够满足峰值负载的服务器，全年放在 InGen 数据中心；方案 B —— 机密数据与常态负载留在 InGen 自有基础设施，年度任务期间临时租用公有云资源；方案 C —— 整体迁到云提供商，但预留固定数量的机器一整年。

(a) Design B 属于哪种云部分收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Classify Design B as private, public, or hybrid cloud. Briefly justify your answer. Expected: 25 words.
题干译文：把 Design B 归类为 private、public 或 hybrid cloud，并简要说明理由。（约 25 词）

笔记定位：01 章有 cloud 定义、五大特征、CapEx/OpEx；但 private / public / hybrid 的三分术语笔记未收录，需自行补齐。

中文回答：Design B 属于 hybrid cloud（混合云）。判据是"资源归谁拥有、谁运维"，不是技术：常态负载与机密基因组数据留在 InGen 自有的 private cloud 上，年度 campaign 期间临时租用 public cloud 资源；两者跨越所有权边界组合起来、协同交付同一项服务，所以是混合形态。纯 public 会把全部负载迁到提供商，纯 private 则全部留在本地。

English answer：Design B is a hybrid cloud. The deployment model is decided by ownership and operational control, not by technology: normal workloads and the confidential genomic data stay on InGen's own private cloud, while the resources needed for the six-week campaign are rented temporarily from a public cloud run by a third party. Because the two parts are combined across the ownership boundary and co-operate to deliver one service, the result is hybrid; moving everything to the provider would be public, and keeping everything in-house would be private.

(b) Design A 与 Design B 的 CapEx / OpEx 比较已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Compare Designs A and B in terms of CapEx and OpEx. Why might Design B be financially attractive when peak demand lasts only six weeks? Expected: 50 words.
题干译文：从 CapEx 与 OpEx 两个角度比较方案 A 与方案 B。当峰值需求只持续六周时，为什么方案 B 在财务上更有吸引力？（约 50 词）

笔记定位：01 章「CapEx 与 OpEx」——"云把 CapEx 转成了 OpEx"；03 章给了 efficiency 与 core-hours 两把尺子。

中文回答：Design A 是 CapEx-heavy（资本性支出重）：t=0 一次性采购服务器、机房、供电与制冷，之后按年折旧。但峰值只有六周——一年 8760 h 中仅约 1008 h 在用，按 efficiency = core-hours used / core-hours allocated 衡量，年利用率仅约 11.5%，设备大部分时间在闲置。Design B 把峰值部分转为 OpEx（运营性支出）：按核时/按小时付费，只用六周就只付六周，支出曲线跟随需求；同时把硬件过时、容量锁死与闲置维护的风险转移给 provider。补充（拿分点）：若峰值年年满载、且云的单位价格溢价高于自建折旧，则长期看 A 更省——结论取决于峰值利用率与云溢价之比。

English answer：Design A is CapEx-heavy: servers, the machine room, power and cooling are bought up front and depreciated over several years. Its weakness is utilisation — the peak lasts only six weeks, i.e. about 1008 of 8760 hours a year (≈11.5% by efficiency = core-hours used / core-hours allocated), so capacity sized for the peak sits idle for most of the year. Design B converts the peak into OpEx: resources are paid for per core-hour only while they are needed, so cost tracks demand, and hardware refresh, capacity lock-in and idle maintenance risk move to the provider. In short, cloud converts CapEx into OpEx. The trade-off is that if the peak ran at full load every year, the cloud price premium could exceed depreciation, making Design A cheaper — the decision hinges on peak utilisation versus that premium.

(c) 让 Design B 特别合适的两条云特征已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Identify two characteristics of cloud computing that make Design B particularly suitable for this workload. Expected: 25 words.
题干译文：指出两条使方案 B 特别适合该负载的云计算特征。（约 25 词）

笔记定位：01 章「五大特征」全在；旁注"故全天候运行不等于 cloud"，判据是整套特征。

中文回答：① rapid elasticity（快速弹性）：六周内把分配量从 20 个处理器扩到 400，campaign 结束后再缩回 20——扩容速度必须快于硬件采购周期，这是自建做不到的。② measured service（可计量服务）：按核时计费，只为实际使用的六周付费，与 (b) 的 OpEx 论证互为支撑。可再补 resource pooling（资源池化）：多租户共享物理资源，用户无需关心那 400 个处理器在哪里，也不必为峰值设备长期持有。注意：只写特征名不解释等于没答，要说明"这条特征对本 workload 意味着什么"。

可替换项（同样得分）：on-demand self-service（按需自助）——研究者无需与 provider 人工交互、无需走采购审批，自己就能把那 400 个处理器开出来，这同样是"六周峰值"能成立的前提。⚠️ 注意两点：① 规范写法是 on-demand self-service（NIST 原词），不要写成 "On demanded self service"；② 它的针对性比 rapid elasticity 弱——单独写 "on-demand self-service + measured service" 会丢掉"六周内扩缩"这个关键条件。最稳的组合仍是 rapid elasticity + measured service，把 on-demand self-service 放在补充句里。

English answer：Two characteristics make Design B particularly suitable. First, rapid elasticity: the allocation can grow from 20 to 400 processors within the six-week campaign and shrink back afterwards, which no hardware procurement cycle can match. Second, measured service: usage is metered and billed per core-hour, so InGen pays only for the campaign — the financial counterpart of the OpEx argument in (b). Resource pooling is also relevant: the 400 processors are drawn from a shared multi-tenant pool, so the researchers neither know nor care which physical machines they get. Note that naming the characteristics without explaining their effect on this workload would not earn full marks.

An acceptable substitute is on-demand self-service: the researchers provision the extra 400 processors themselves, with no human interaction with the provider and no procurement approval, which is equally a precondition for meeting a six-week peak. Two cautions: the correct term is on-demand self-service, not "on demanded self service"; and it is less specific than rapid elasticity, since it says nothing about growing and shrinking within the campaign. The safest pair is therefore rapid elasticity + measured service, with on-demand self-service offered as a supporting point.

(d) "在云里就一定高效"错在哪部分收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：A manager claims: “Design C must be efficient because the machines are in the cloud.” Explain why using a cloud provider does not automatically guarantee efficient resource utilisation. Expected: 50 words.
题干译文：一位经理声称：“方案 C 一定高效，因为机器在云里。”解释为什么使用云提供商并不自动保证资源的高效利用。（约 50 词）

笔记定位：笔记没有这句反驳，但 03 章的 efficiency、Utilisation 与执行时间模型 Ttotal ≈ Tparallelwork/P + Tserial + Tcommunication + Timbalance（"后面三项都是加不掉的税"）就是本问的论证骨架。

中文回答：云只改变"资源归谁、怎么付费"，没有改变"资源要被用满才高效"。① 预留的机器闲置也计费：Design C 固定预留一整年，core-hours allocated 的分母一年都下不来，而分子只有六周的量，效率仍约 11.5%——和 Design A 一样难看。② 效率取决于软件能否吃满：efficiency 的分子来自真实负载，单线程程序放进 64 核云主机，分母涨 64 倍、分子不变，效率跌到 1.6%。③ 池化的代价进入时间模型：跨物理机/跨机架的网络抖动、超卖与"吵闹邻居"、I/O 争用，全部落进 Tcommunication 与 Timbalance 这两项"税"。④ 固定预留等于放弃了弹性：Design C 本质上是"用 OpEx 的形式买下 CapEx 的容量"，把云最有价值的那条特征关掉。一句话：云提供的是 capability（能拿到资源），高效来自 utilisation（有没有用满），两者不是一回事。

English answer：Being in the cloud changes who owns the resources and how they are billed; it does not change the requirement to keep them busy. The reserved machines in Design C are paid for all year, so core-hours allocated never falls while actual use is confined to six weeks — efficiency stays around 11.5%, exactly as in Design A. Efficiency also depends on the software: a single-threaded program on a 64-core cloud host multiplies the denominator by 64 and leaves the numerator unchanged. Pooling adds its own costs, which appear as Tcommunication and Timbalance in Ttotal ≈ Tparallelwork/P + Tserial + Tcommunication + Timbalance — network jitter across racks, over-subscription and noisy neighbours, and I/O contention. Finally, a fixed annual reservation is simply CapEx capacity bought through an OpEx contract, discarding the elasticity that made cloud attractive. In one sentence: the cloud supplies capability, efficiency comes from utilisation, and the two are not the same thing.

(e) 云可扩展性 vs 软件并行性已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：During the campaign, InGen increases its allocation from 20 processors to 400 processors, but the genome-analysis program still uses only one processor. Explain the difference between cloud scalability and software parallelism. Expected: 50 words.
题干译文：任务期间 InGen 把分配量从 20 个处理器增加到 400 个，但基因组分析程序仍只使用一个处理器。解释 cloud scalability 与 software parallelism 的区别。（约 50 词）

笔记定位：02 章数据/任务并行、06 章 A = T₁/T∞ 与 σ = A/P（"σ < 1：核心闲置"）、Amdahl 定律、03 章执行时间模型。

中文回答：scalability / elasticity 是供给侧属性：20 → 400 改变的是 worker 数 P，是 T(P, N) 里 P 那一维。parallelism 是需求侧属性：程序自身能同时跑多少 task，由依赖图与关键路径决定，度量是平均并行度 A = T₁/T∞。本题程序只用一个处理器，A ≈ 1。于是并行松弛度 σ = A/P = 1/400 ≪ 1——笔记原话"核心闲置"：多出来的处理器只在计费，不在干活。时间模型上 Ttotal = Tparallelwork/P + Tserial + Tcomm + Timbalance，100% 串行时第一项缩不下去，加速比 → 1；Amdahl 视角即 s ≈ 1，S(P) → 1，绝对上限 1/s = 1。

English answer：Scalability, or elasticity, is a supply-side property: increasing the allocation from 20 to 400 processors only changes P in T(P, N). Parallelism is a demand-side property: how many tasks the program can actually execute concurrently, bounded by its dependency graph and critical path, measured by the average parallelism A = T₁/T∞. Here the program runs on one processor, so A ≈ 1 and the parallel slackness σ = A/P = 1/400 ≪ 1 — the notes' phrase is idle cores. The extra processors are billed but not working. In the time model Ttotal = Tparallelwork/P + Tserial + Tcommunication + Timbalance, the first term cannot shrink for a fully serial program, so speedup tends to 1; equivalently, Amdahl's law with s ≈ 1 gives S(P) → 1, the absolute speedup limit 1/s = 1. Cloud scalability provides the processors; only the software can use them.

Q3内存容量、MPI 与集群构成 · Memory capacity, MPI and cluster anatomy

STEMQuestion stem（原题）：Tony Stark develops a simulation that requires approximately 600 GB of memory. A new workstation contains 64 CPU cores and 128 GB RAM. He considers two strategies: Strategy A — use OpenMP on one workstation. Strategy B — use MPI across eight workstations.
题干译文：Tony Stark 开发的仿真需要约 600 GB 内存。一台新工作站有 64 个 CPU 核心与 128 GB 内存。他考虑两种策略：策略 A —— 在单台工作站上用 OpenMP；策略 B —— 跨 8 台工作站用 MPI。

(a) 64 核 OpenMP 为什么解决不了根本问题部分收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Explain why simply using all 64 CPU cores with OpenMP does not solve the fundamental problem. Expected: 50 words.
题干译文：解释为什么仅用 OpenMP 把 64 个核心全部用上并不能解决根本问题。（约 50 词）

笔记定位：笔记没有这句结论，但 03 章「OpenMP：单节点内」（"通过共享变量与内存交换数据"）与「作业能否放下」（"申请 CPU ≤ 空闲 CPU 且 申请内存 ≤ 空闲内存"）直接支撑。

中文回答：① OpenMP 是单节点共享内存模型：64 个线程共享同一块 128 GB 物理内存，加线程只是加"读这份内存的手"，不增加内存容量。② 按"作业能否放下"判据：申请内存 600 GB > 节点空闲内存 128 GB → 该 job 从一开始就放不进这个节点，问题在容量（memory-bound）而非算力。③ 强行跑的后果：必然触发 swap/paging 与 thrashing，有效带宽骤降，时间模型里表现为 Tserial 与 Timbalance 暴涨，Tparallelwork/P 缩得再多也救不回来。④ Amdahl 视角：串行占比 s 由算法与数据布局决定，加核改不了 s。

English answer：OpenMP is a shared-memory, single-node model: all 64 threads share the same 128 GB of physical memory, so adding cores adds consumers of that memory but not capacity. Applying the fit test — requested memory ≤ free memory on the node — a 600 GB dataset exceeds the 128 GB available, so the job does not fit on that node at all; the problem is capacity (it is memory-bound), not arithmetic throughput. If it is forced to run, the working set spills to swap and the process thrashes, which inflates Tserial and Timbalance in Ttotal ≈ Tparallelwork/P + Tserial + Tcommunication + Timbalance and destroys the benefit of the parallel term. From Amdahl's perspective, the serial fraction is fixed by the algorithm and data layout, so extra cores cannot remove it.

(c) 为什么 MPI 更自然已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Explain why MPI is a more natural programming model for Strategy B. Expected: 50 words.
题干译文：解释为什么 MPI 是策略 B 更自然的编程模型。（约 50 词）

笔记定位：03 章「MPI：跨节点」——"将数据拆分为多个节点上的各个进程"；「OpenMP：单节点内」；04 章 SPMD。

中文回答：① 模型匹配：MPI 是 SPMD + 分布式内存，每个 rank 各持一份数据；OpenMP 的共享内存语义跨机器不成立（没有跨机器的一致地址空间），跨节点只能靠显式消息传递。② 数据布局天然吻合：600 GB 本来就要按 5 份切开（domain decomposition），一个 rank 对应一个节点，各自只看得到自己那块。③ 通信可控：跨节点没有缓存一致性的"魔法"，所有数据流动都必须显式写出（halo 交换、集合通信），因此可以用 T ≈ α + nβ 预测与优化——既是负担也是优点。

English answer：MPI is a SPMD, distributed-memory model in which each rank holds its own partition of the data, whereas OpenMP's shared-memory semantics do not extend across machines because there is no cross-node coherent address space; across nodes, data can only move through explicit messages. The data layout matches naturally as well: 600 GB must be split into five partitions anyway, and one rank per node sees only its own block. Communication also becomes explicit and therefore controllable — halo exchanges and collective operations are written in the code, so their cost can be estimated with T ≈ α + nβ and optimised, which is both a burden and an advantage.

English answer：An MPI job must be scheduled all-or-nothing. English answer：Neither value is read from the code; both follow from the stem, which states that the program is executed with exactly two processes. No line in the program sets the process count — MPI_Init only consumes the command line, so the count comes from the run configuration (mpirun -n 2).

A useful extra point: a rank is a logical identifier, and which physical node or core receives which rank is decided by the runtime and is not guaranteed, so a rank must not be treated as a machine number.

MPI_COMM_WORLD is the global communicator: it contains every process started by this run, it is the default context for point-to-point and collective communication, it defines the group within which messages are matched, and subgroups can later be formed with MPI_Comm_split.

(b) 为什么会死锁已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Explain why the program deadlocks. Expected: 75 words.
题干译文：解释该程序为什么会死锁。（约 75 词）

笔记定位：04 章「阻塞、死锁与非阻塞」——"Send / Recv 均需等本地操作完成后才返回…若收发双方都在等待对方，就触发了死锁"。

中文回答：① MPI_Recv 是阻塞（blocking）调用：只有消息真正到达并写入缓冲区才返回。② 两个进程都是先 Recv、后 Send：rank 0 等 rank 1，rank 1 等 rank 0。③ 形成循环等待（circular wait）：各自的 Send 都要等自己的 Recv 返回，而 Recv 又在等对方的 Send → 没有进程能推进 → 永久挂起。④ 精确性提醒：不要说"Send 一定阻塞"——标准模式下 MPI_Send 可能被 eager 协议缓冲后立即返回，真正致命的是"两边都先 Recv"这个顺序。⑤ 把顺序改成"rank 0 先 Send、rank 1 先 Recv"能跑通，但那只是恰好装得下缓冲区，消息一大就会重新死锁——这种"碰巧能跑"正是不该依赖的写法。

English answer：MPI_Recv is a blocking call: it returns only when the message has arrived and been copied into the receive buffer. Both processes call MPI_Recv before MPI_Send, so rank 0 waits for a message from rank 1 while rank 1 waits for a message from rank 0. This is a circular wait: each process's send depends on its own receive returning, and each receive depends on the other process's send, so no process can make progress and the program hangs. A precise answer should not claim that MPI_Send always blocks — in standard mode it may be buffered by an eager protocol and return immediately — the fatal factor is the ordering, with both ranks receiving first. Reversing the order so that rank 0 sends first would appear to work, but only because the message happens to fit in the buffer; larger messages would deadlock again, which is why such code must not be relied upon.

(c) 用 MPI_Sendrecv 改写未收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Rewrite the exchange using MPI_Sendrecv so that it cannot deadlock. Expected: 25 words + 5–8 lines of code.

笔记定位：笔记未收录 MPI_Sendrecv；笔记给的死锁替代方案是非阻塞通信 MPI_Isend / MPI_Irecv + MPI_Waitall（04 章与 halo 例子）。下面这段逐参数解读不属于笔记内容，是为了让代码"看得懂、写得出"而补的。

中文回答：先把 MPI_Sendrecv 的签名拆开看——它共 12 个参数，就是"一组发送（5 个）+ 一组接收（5 个）+ 两个公共参数"拼起来的：

MPI_Sendrecv(sendbuf, sendcount, sendtype, dest,   sendtag,    /* 发送侧 5 个，与 MPI_Send 同序 */
             recvbuf, recvcount, recvtype, source, recvtag,    /* 接收侧 5 个，与 MPI_Recv 同序 */
             comm, status);                                    /* 两个公共参数 */

为什么它不会死锁：原程序死锁的根因是"两边都先阻塞在 Recv"。MPI_Sendrecv 把"发"与"收"绑进同一次调用，标准要求通信子系统自己处理两者的先后（the communication subsystem takes care of these issues）——实现可以用非阻塞收发或内部缓冲推进，因此不存在"我等你发、你等我发"的循环等待。

逐行对应（右侧注释即变量含义）：

int other   = 1 - rank;                 /* 对话的另一方：rank0→1，rank1→0 */
int sendval = (rank == 0) ? 10 : 20;    /* 复现原程序：rank0 发 10，rank1 发 20 */
int recvval = 0;                        /* 收件箱：必须与 sendval 分开，标准禁止 sendbuf/recvbuf 重叠 */

MPI_Sendrecv(&sendval, 1, MPI_INT, other, 0,   /* 发：把 sendval 发给 other，tag 0 */
             &recvval, 1, MPI_INT, other, 0,   /* 收：从 other 收进 recvval，tag 0 */
             MPI_COMM_WORLD, MPI_STATUS_IGNORE);

English answer：The signature MPI_Sendrecv(sendbuf, sendcount, sendtype, dest, sendtag, recvbuf, recvcount, recvtype, source, recvtag, comm, status) is simply a send half and a receive half joined together. The first five arguments appear in exactly the same order as in MPI_Send, the next five exactly as in MPI_Recv, and the last two — communicator and status — are shared.

Mapping the rewritten code: &sendval is the sendbuf holding the value to transmit, 1 the sendcount, MPI_INT the sendtype, other the destination, 0 the sendtag; &recvval is the recvbuf that receives the partner's value, 1 the recvcount, MPI_INT the recvtype, other the source, 0 the recvtag; and MPI_COMM_WORLD with MPI_STATUS_IGNORE close the call. other = 1 - rank names the partner, so rank 0 talks to rank 1 and vice versa, and sendval reproduces the original values, rank 0 sending 10 and rank 1 sending 20.

It cannot deadlock because the original program failed by having both ranks block in a receive first. Taking the two lines in the program, rank 0 sends MPI_Send(&value, 1, MPI_INT, 1, 0, MPI_COMM_WORLD), in which the fourth argument gives destination = 1, the fifth tag = 0 and the sixth communicator = MPI_COMM_WORLD; and rank 0 receives MPI_Recv(&value, 1, MPI_INT, 1, 0, MPI_COMM_WORLD, MPI_STATUS_IGNORE), in which the fourth argument gives source = 1, the fifth tag = 0 and the sixth communicator = MPI_COMM_WORLD, followed by MPI_STATUS_IGNORE. The send is addressed to rank 1 with tag 0 and the receive expects rank 1 with tag 0 in the same communicator, so the sender's (communicator, destination, tag) matches the receiver's (communicator, source, tag) and the message is delivered.

Their purposes: the destination names the rank the message is addressed to, while the source restricts which rank a receive will accept, with MPI_ANY_SOURCE as the wildcard and the real sender reported in status.MPI_SOURCE. The tag is a user-defined message-type identifier that must agree on both sides; it separates different kinds of traffic inside one communicator — halo data with tag 0, results with tag 1 — and so prevents mismatches, with MPI_ANY_TAG as the wildcard. The communicator is the context in which matching happens, which makes it an isolation boundary between modules or subgroups. A message is identified by the triple (communicator, source, tag): if rank 1 sent with tag 5, rank 0's tag-0 receive would wait forever, which is the easiest way to produce an artificial deadlock.

(c) 为什么通常只有 rank 0 能用最终值已收录

(e) 集合通信为什么优于手写 Send/Recv部分收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Why is a collective operation generally preferable to manually implementing the same reduction using many MPI_Send and MPI_Recv calls? Expected: 25 words.
题干译文：为什么用集合操作通常优于用大量 MPI_Send 与 MPI_Recv 手动实现同样的归约？（约 25 词）

笔记定位：笔记有集合通信定义（"一个函数调用，多个进程参与"）与 T ≈ α + nβ（"小消息几乎全被 α 支配"），但无"手写 vs 集合通信"的直接对比。

中文回答：① 算法：集合通信由实现选择树形 / 递归倍增（recursive doubling）算法，深度约 log P；手写"root 逐个收"是 P−1 轮串行等待。② 延迟：T ≈ α + nβ，P−1 轮意味着 (P−1)·α 的启动开销，小消息时几乎全被 α 吃掉。③ 正确性：集合通信把"所有进程必须参与"、缓冲区不得重叠、类型与 tag 匹配这些坑封在库里，手写极易踩死锁与错配。④ 可优化：实现可用拓扑感知选路、网络内归约（如 SHARP）、与计算重叠，用户代码无法触及。⑤ 可维护：意图明确，替换算法（Reduce → Allreduce）只改一行。

English answer：Five reasons. Algorithmically, the library picks tree or recursive-doubling schedules of depth about log P, whereas a hand-written reduction needs P−1 sequential rounds at the root. In terms of latency, T ≈ α + nβ means those P−1 rounds cost (P−1)·α of start-up overhead, which dominates for small messages. Correctness is safer: the requirement that all processes participate, the ban on overlapping buffers and the matching of datatypes and tags are all handled inside the library, whereas hand-written code readily deadlocks or mismatches. Performance can be tuned below the API — topology-aware routing, in-network reduction such as SHARP, and overlap with computation — none of which user code can reach. Finally, the intent is explicit and swapping the algorithm, for instance reduce to allreduce, is a one-line change.

Q7OpenMP 竞态与数据作用域 · OpenMP race conditions and data scope

STEMQuestion stem（原题）：A student parallelises the following loop. The program becomes faster, but different executions sometimes produce different answers.
题干译文：一名学生把下面的循环并行化。程序确实变快了，但不同次运行有时会给出不同的结果。

}

(a) 竞态 + 两次丢更新已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Identify the problem and explain how two threads can lose an update to total. Expected: 50 words.
题干译文：指出问题所在，并解释两个线程如何丢失一次对 total 的更新。（约 50 词）

笔记定位：05 章「竞态与四种同步手段」——"在 fork 出来的线程进行写入时，可能会相互覆盖"；03 章"同一块内存大家都能读写——这也是竞态条件的根源"。

中文回答：total += values[i] 被编译器拆成读—改—写三步（tmp = total；tmp = tmp + values[i]；total = tmp），三步不是原子操作，构成 race condition（竞态条件）。丢更新（lost update） 的时序（total 初值 10，线程 A 加 5、线程 B 加 7）：A 读到 tmpA = 10 → 切换到 B，B 也读到 tmpB = 10（A 尚未写回）→ B 写回 total = 17 → A 写回 total = 15，B 的更新被覆盖；正确结果 22，实得 15。为什么结果时好时坏：线程在哪个指令处切换、循环怎么切块，由运行时与 OS 调度决定 → 非确定性（non-determinism）。为什么还变快了：并行是真的，只是正确性坏了——快而错，且小规模时常常"看起来正常"，最难查。

English answer：The statement total += values[i] is compiled into three non-atomic steps — read total into a temporary, add values[i], write the temporary back — so concurrent threads update a shared variable without synchronisation, which is a race condition. A lost update occurs as follows: with total initially 10, thread A reads 10 into its temporary, then is pre-empted; thread B also reads 10, adds 7 and writes back 17; thread A resumes, adds 5 to the stale 10 and writes back 15, destroying B's contribution. The correct answer is 22, the observed value 15. Different runs produce different answers because pre-emption points and loop chunking depend on the OpenMP runtime and the OS scheduler, making the result non-deterministic. The program is genuinely faster, but it is fast and wrong — the classic failure that hides in small runs.

(b) 用最合适的机制改写已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Rewrite the loop using the most appropriate OpenMP mechanism. Expected: 5–6 lines of code.
题干译文：用最合适的 OpenMP 机制重写该循环。（约 5–6 行代码）

笔记定位：05 章「① Reduction 归约」——"开销最低，适用于合并变量"，"每个线程各自攒一份局部结果，退出时自动合并，不需要手动加锁"。

中文回答：用 reduction 子句：每个线程拿一份私有副本各自累加，到 join 屏障时按 + 合并。它既消除竞态，又不产生锁竞争；在笔记的手段对比表里开销最低、保护范围是整个变量，正好匹配"循环求累加"。

    total += values[i];
}

English answer：Use the reduction clause. Each thread accumulates into a private copy of total, and the copies are combined with + at the implicit join barrier. This removes the race without introducing lock contention, and the reduction clause has the lowest overhead of the available mechanisms because it protects the whole variable in one place — exactly the right match for a loop accumulating a sum.

(c) 为什么 critical 正确但不合适已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Why would “#pragma omp critical” applied to total += values[i]; produce a correct result but normally be less appropriate for this operation? Expected: 50 words.
题干译文：为什么在 total += values[i]; 上加 #pragma omp critical 能得到正确结果，但通常不如其他方案合适？（约 50 词）

笔记定位：05 章「③ critical 临界区」——"同一时间只允许一个 thread 进入"，代价："临界区代码容易退化为串行——保护的范围越大，并行度就损失得越多"。

中文回答：critical 保证互斥，所以结果正确，但代价是粒度灾难：n 次迭代就要进出临界区 n 次、加解锁 n 次，而真正加进 total 的计算只有一次浮点加法（纳秒级），锁的开销远大于它。其次完全串行化：临界区一次只许一个线程进，这个循环的并行部分被压成一条串行队列，Tparallelwork/P 缩不下去（等价于把 s 抬到接近 1，Amdahl 退化成 1×）。再加上线程排队等待与缓存行在核间弹跳，Timbalance 与开销上升。对比：reduction 是"每线程局部副本、只在结束时合并一次"，critical 是"每次更新都排队一次"，次数差 n 倍。

English answer：A critical section is correct because it guarantees mutual exclusion, but its granularity is wrong for this operation. Each of the n iterations enters and leaves the critical section and acquires and releases a lock, while the protected work is a single floating-point addition, so the synchronisation costs far more than the operation it protects. The loop is also fully serialised: only one thread may be inside the critical section at a time, so the parallel term cannot shrink and Amdahl's law degenerates towards a speedup of 1. Waiting threads and cache lines bouncing between cores add further Timbalance and overhead. A reduction accumulates in per-thread private copies and combines them once at the end, whereas critical forces n serialised updates.

(d) values / total / i 的作用域已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：In the corrected implementation, classify the following: values, total, i. Expected: 10–20 words.
题干译文：在修正后的实现中，为以下变量分类：values、total、i。（约 10–20 词）

笔记定位：05 章「数据作用域」四类——shared"所有 threads 共享，例 a[n][n]"；private"每个 thread 独立副本值，例循环里那个 i"；reduction"每个 thread 独立执行，最后归约，例 total"。

中文回答：values → shared（只被读、不被写，所有线程读同一份最省内存也最快）。total → reduction（被所有线程累加，需私有副本 + 末尾按 + 合并）。i → private（循环控制变量，每线程必须有自己的计数；parallel for 的循环变量默认即 private）。防错提示：若写成 #pragma omp parallel 手动分区而 i 又在循环外声明，则 i 默认 shared，会再造成一次竞态。

English answer：values is shared: it is only read, never written, so one copy serves all threads. total is a reduction variable, since every thread accumulates into it and the private copies are combined with + at the end. i is private: it is the loop control variable and each thread needs its own counter, which is the default for the variable of a parallel for. Note that if the loop were parallelised with a plain parallel region and i were declared outside the loop, i would default to shared and introduce a second race.

(e) 为什么 default(none) 更安全未收录

(f) firstprivate(scale) 做什么已收录

题干译文：假设声明 double scale = 0.5; 在并行区内 firstprivate(scale) 子句会做什么？（约 25 词）

STEMQuestion stem（原题）：An MPI program contains the following timing code. For the same problem size, the results in the table are measured.

(a) 为什么计时前放 MPI_Barrier已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Why is MPI_Barrier placed before the timer? Expected: 25 words.
题干译文：为什么把 MPI_Barrier 放在计时器之前？（约 25 词）

笔记定位：04 章补充函数——"MPI_Barrier：计时前同步所有进程——保证起跑线一致"。

中文回答：MPI 进程的启动、初始化与读输入耗时彼此不同；若各自 MPI_Wtime() 就开始计时，先到的进程会把等待时间算进自己的计时，测到的是含启动抖动的墙钟时间而非 solve() 的真实耗时。MPI_Barrier 让所有进程对齐到同一时刻再取 start，各进程的 local_time 才可比，MPI_MAX 才能取到真正的瓶颈进程。严格来说，Barrier 之后由一个进程取 start 再广播最精确。

English answer：Process start-up, initialisation and input reading take different amounts of time on different ranks, so if each rank calls MPI_Wtime() independently the earlier ones include their waiting time in their own measurement, and what is measured is a wall-clock interval containing start-up jitter rather than the duration of solve(). A barrier brings every rank to a common instant before the timer is read, which makes the per-rank intervals comparable so that MPI_MAX identifies the true bottleneck. For maximum rigour one rank should read the start time after the barrier and broadcast it.

(b) 为什么用 MPI_MAX 而不是 SUM 或平均已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Why is MPI_MAX used instead of MPI_SUM or an average? Expected: 25 words.
题干译文：为什么使用 MPI_MAX 而不是 MPI_SUM 或平均值？（约 25 词）

笔记定位：04 章——"MPI_MAX：获取最慢进程的运行时间——并行程序的总耗时由最慢的那个决定"。

中文回答：MAX → "墙钟时间是多少"：所有进程同时开跑、全部结束才算完，整体耗时 = max(local_time)，这是唯一有物理意义的量。SUM 错在：各进程是同时在跑的，时间不能相加；P 个进程各跑 14 s 总耗时仍是 14 s，不是 14P 秒（SUM 得到的是 CPU 总时间，属另一指标）。AVG 错在：平均值会掩盖最慢者——一个进程 14 s、其余 8 s 时平均只有 9 s，严重低估真实耗时，也掩盖了 Timbalance 这个瓶颈。

English answer：MPI_MAX answers the right question, namely what the wall-clock time was: the processes run concurrently and the run ends when the last one finishes, so the elapsed time is the maximum of the per-rank times. MPI_SUM is wrong because concurrent times cannot be added — P ranks each taking 14 s take 14 s in total, not 14P s; a sum measures aggregate CPU time, which is a different quantity. An average is wrong because it conceals the slowest rank: with one rank at 14 s and the rest at 8 s the mean is 9 s, which badly underestimates the real duration and hides the load imbalance. The parallel runtime is determined by the slowest participant.

(c) 计算 S(16)已收录

(e) 这是强缩放还是弱缩放已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Is this a strong-scaling or weak-scaling experiment? Expected: 75 words.

English answer：This is a strong-scaling experiment. The criterion is whether the problem size changes, and the question states that the problem size is the same while P goes from 1 to 4, 8 and 16, which is Amdahl's fixed-workload setting. The evidence matches: the times fall from 120 to 34, 19 and 14 s, giving speedups of 3.53, 6.32 and 8.57 and efficiencies of 88%, 79% and 53.6%, so efficiency decreases monotonically with P. Strong scaling asks how much faster one fixed problem can run and necessarily converges to the absolute limit 1/s; the falling curve reflects insufficient problem size rather than a poor implementation. A weak-scaling experiment would instead keep the work per processor constant, with N(P) = P·N₁, and check whether the runtime stays roughly constant, which is the second experiment reported in (h).

English answer：Two main causes, both visible in Ttotal ≈ Tparallelwork/P + Tserial + Tcommunication + Timbalance. 中文回答：① 通信模型直接吃亏：T ≈ α + nβ。跨站点把 α 从微秒抬到毫秒级 RTT，β 被公网限制；MPI 每步都要交换 halo/做集合通信，每步多几毫秒 × 上百万步 = 灾难。② 同步放大最慢者：每个 MPI_Barrier 与集合通信都等最慢者，WAN 抖动与丢包让"最慢者"变成随机变量 → Timbalance 与 Tcommunication 暴涨。③ 失效与异构：跨域没有统一调度，站点随时可能被收回（见 (c)）；一个进程掉线整个作业直接失败，不像独立作业可以重试。④ 对比独立作业：无需通信 → 延迟无关、可任意切分重跑、天然容错，正是 Grid 的设计目标（HTC + throughput）。结论：这是"把 HPC 负载放到 HTC 载体上"——载体与负载形态错配。若必须跨站点，需要专网、把通信域限制在单站点内（站点内 MPI + 站点间极少通信的层级化并行），或换算法降低通信频率。

English answer：This is an HPC workload placed on an HTC carrier. Tightly coupled MPI depends on low-latency, predictable communication, but geographically separated sites raise α from microseconds to milliseconds of round-trip time and cap β at wide-area bandwidth. Every halo exchange and collective operation pays that latency, so a few extra milliseconds per step multiplied by millions of steps dominates the runtime. Synchronisation also amplifies the slowest participant: each barrier and collective waits for the last rank to arrive, and WAN jitter and loss make that arrival time unpredictable, inflating Tcommunication and Timbalance. Failure behaviour compounds the problem, since a single lost rank normally aborts the whole job, whereas independent jobs can simply be retried. Independent jobs, by contrast, do not communicate, so they are latency-insensitive, freely partitionable and inherently fault tolerant — exactly what a grid is designed for. If cross-site coupling is unavoidable, the remedies are a dedicated low-latency network, hierarchical parallelism that confines MPI communication within a site and communicates rarely between sites, or an algorithm with much lower communication frequency.

STEMQuestion stem（原题）：Ethan Hunt launches a scientific grid job that will execute for 36 hours. A colleague suggests copying Ethan's long-term private key onto the remote compute nodes so that the job can authenticate to other services later.

(a) 公钥与私钥的区别已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Explain the difference between a public key and a private key in asymmetric cryptography. Expected: 25 words.
题干译文：解释非对称密码学中公钥与私钥的区别。（约 25 词）

笔记定位：08 章「密钥对」表——Public key 公开分享 .pub；Private key 必须保密；分工：加密数据/验证签名用公钥，解密数据/创建签名用私钥。

中文回答：一对数学上配对、用途上互补的密钥。公钥（public key，后缀 .pub）：可公开分发，用于加密数据（发给所有者）与验证签名。私钥（private key）：必须保密，用于解密收到的数据与创建数字签名。两点易错：① 公钥本身被看到没关系，被换掉才是灾难——所以"公钥与身份的绑定"也需要保护，这正是 PKI 的理由。② 私钥通常是硬件载体，禁止发送给他人，"私钥一旦离身，整套身份就归别人了"。

English answer：A key pair consists of two mathematically related keys with complementary uses. The public key, conventionally with the .pub suffix, is distributed openly and is used to encrypt data for its owner and to verify digital signatures. The private key must remain secret and is used to decrypt received data and to create digital signatures. Two points are easily confused. First, a public key being seen is harmless, but a public key being replaced is a disaster, so the binding between key and identity must itself be protected — which is the reason PKI exists. Second, a private key usually resides in a hardware token and must never be sent to anyone, since once it leaves its owner the entire identity belongs to whoever holds it.

(b) 为什么绝不能把长期私钥拷到远端已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Why should Ethan never copy or share his long-term private key with the remote machine? Expected: 25 words.
题干译文：为什么 Ethan 绝不应把长期私钥拷贝或共享给远端机器？（约 25 词）

笔记定位：08 章「两条容易搞错的」②——"私钥通常是硬件载体，禁止发送给他人，否则就失去了意义"；Proxy 段答题提示要求"说明保护 private key 的重要性 + 分发 private key 的缺点"。

中文回答：① 身份被永久劫持：私钥就是身份；拷到共享计算节点（最不可信的地方：root、恶意同租户、磁盘残留）＝身份离开掌控，攻击者能以 Ethan 的名义登录任何接受该身份的 Grid 服务。② 不可撤销、影响面极大：这是长期密钥，绑定所有服务而非某一个作业；撤销要走 CA 的吊销流程（CRL/OCSP），代价极高，且已发生的访问无法回滚。③ 归责失效：日志上所有操作都记成 "Ethan 做的"，无法区分真人与攻击者，直接摧毁 Accounting & audit 的可信度。④ 违反最小权限/委托原则：36 小时的作业只需要"跑这一件事"的权限，不需要全部权限——Delegation 委托 的要求正是"权限与使用时间受限"。正确做法：用 proxy credential（见 (f)），长期私钥留在本地。

English answer：The private key is the identity, so copying it to a remote node hands the identity to a machine he does not control — and a shared compute node is the least trustworthy place, with root access, untrusted co-tenants and residual data on disk. Anyone who compromises that node can then authenticate to every grid service as Ethan. Because the key is long-term it is bound to all services rather than to one 36-hour job, so the blast radius is the whole Grid; revocation requires the CA's revocation machinery (CRL or OCSP), is slow and disruptive, and cannot undo access that has already happened. Accountability also collapses, since every action in the audit trail is attributed to Ethan and an attacker is indistinguishable from him, which undermines the Accounting & audit requirement. This also violates least privilege and delegation: a single job needs permission to run that job, with rights and duration restricted, not the user's entire authority. The correct mechanism is a short-lived proxy credential.

(c) 什么是 PKI已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：What is a Public Key Infrastructure (PKI)? Expected: 25 words.
题干译文：什么是公钥基础设施（PKI）？（约 25 词）

笔记定位：08 章——"PKI：一个 connecting public keys to trusted identities 的体系，帮助使用者确认某把公钥确实属于它所声称的网站、组织"。

中文回答：PKI（Public Key Infrastructure，公钥基础设施） 是"把公钥绑定到可信身份"的一整套体系，解决的问题不是加密而是信任传递："我怎么知道这把公钥真属于 hud.ac.uk 而不是中间人被换给我的？" 它包含：角色（CA、RA、持有者、依赖方）、数据结构（X.509 证书、CRL 吊销列表）、流程/协议（签发、分发、验证、吊销、更新）。答案的核心句：信任 CA，于是可以信任 CA 签过的东西。

English answer：PKI — Public Key Infrastructure — is the framework that connects public keys to trusted identities, and it solves trust distribution rather than encryption: how can a relying party know that a public key really belongs to the organisation it claims, rather than having been substituted by a man in the middle? It comprises roles such as the certification authority, the registration authority, the subject and the relying party; data structures such as X.509 certificates and revocation lists; and the processes of issuing, distributing, validating, revoking and renewing credentials. In one sentence: you trust the CA, and therefore you can trust what the CA has signed.

(d) CA 在 PKI 中的作用已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：What role does a Certificate Authority (CA) play in a PKI? Expected: 25 words.
题干译文：证书颁发机构（CA）在 PKI 中扮演什么角色？（约 25 词）

笔记定位：08 章图 8-2 注——"CA 验证身份后签发证书：确定'身份与公钥'的绑定可以被信赖"。

中文回答：① 验证身份（identity proofing）：签发前核实申请者确实是它声称的主体。② 签发（sign）：用自己的私钥对证书内容做数字签名，任何人可用 CA 的公钥验证。③ 定义约束：写入 validity（有效期）与 constraints（用途/域名/路径约束），限制这张证书能干什么。④ 生命周期管理：私钥泄露或身份变更时，通过 CRL/OCSP 让证书提前失效。⑤ 它是信任锚（trust anchor）：整个体系的安全性最终落在"CA 的私钥不泄露"上——所以 CA 被攻破是 PKI 的灾难级事件。

English answer：The certification authority verifies a subject's identity before issuing anything, signs the certificate with its own private key so that anyone holding the CA's public key can validate it, and defines the validity period and constraints such as permitted usage, domains or path length that limit what the certificate may be used for. It also manages the lifecycle by revoking certificates through CRL or OCSP when a key is compromised or an identity changes. The CA is the trust anchor of the whole system: security ultimately reduces to the secrecy of the CA's private key, which is why compromise of a CA is a catastrophic event for a PKI.

(e) X.509 绑定什么，CA 签名为何重要已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：What does an X.509 certificate bind together, and why is the CA's signature important? Expected: 50 words.
题干译文：X.509 证书把什么绑定在一起？CA 的签名为什么重要？（约 50 词）

笔记定位：08 章「X.509 certification」——"一份公开的、被 CA 数字签名的声明，绑定身份与公钥，并说明 validity 与 constraints"；图 8-2 三块拼图 identity + Public key + Restriction → CA signature。

中文回答：X.509 证书绑定四件东西：① identity（身份）——主体是谁（站点名/用户名/主机名）；② public key（公钥）——要绑到该身份上的那把公钥；③ restriction / constraints + validity（限制与有效期）——能用于什么、能用于哪些域、何时过期；④ CA signature（CA 签名）——把以上内容封成不可篡改的声明。CA 签名为什么重要：没有签名，证书只是一张自述，任何人都能生成"我是 hud.ac.uk + 我的公钥"，中间人攻击就此成立；签名覆盖全部字段，改一个字节就验不过，防的是"公钥被换掉"这种真正的灾难；同时让信任可传递——依赖方只需信任 CA 公钥，无需与每个站点事先建立信任（解决 n² 信任配置问题）；签名与有效期、约束共同限制爆炸半径。

English answer：An X.509 certificate binds four things: an identity, that is the subject — a hostname, site or username; the public key to be bound to that identity; the restrictions recorded in the constraints and validity fields, which limit what the certificate may be used for, for which domains, and until when; and the CA's signature, which seals all of the above into a tamper-evident statement. The signature matters because without it a certificate is merely a self-declaration — anyone could assert "I am hud.ac.uk and this is my key" and man-in-the-middle attacks would succeed. Because the signature covers every field, changing a single byte invalidates it, so the real danger, a substituted public key, is prevented. It also makes trust transitive: a relying party only has to trust the CA's public key rather than pre-establishing trust with every site, avoiding quadratic trust configuration. Together with validity and constraints, it bounds the damage a leaked certificate can do.

(f) 为什么短命 proxy 更安全（优点 + 代价成对讲）已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Explain why a short-lived proxy credential is safer for the long-running grid job than Ethan's long-term private key. Expected: 50 words.
题干译文：解释为什么对长时间运行的 grid 作业而言，短生命周期的 proxy 凭据比 Ethan 的长期私钥更安全。（约 50 词）

笔记定位：08 章「Proxy 证书」——short lifetime、"Proxy 有自己的私钥签名…故不需要用户私钥…把长期私钥留在本地，只放出一把短命的替身"；并明确答题提示："优点和代价要成对讲，只讲一半不算答完"。

中文回答：优点：① 不暴露长期私钥——36 小时的作业只需在远端持有一把替身，长期私钥始终留在本地/硬件令牌。② 短寿命 = 自动失效——泄露窗口被压缩到作业时长量级，作业结束钥匙自然作废，无需有人来撤销。③ 权限可缩小（受限委托）——proxy 可写入更小权限与约束（只能访问某 VO 的某几个服务），符合 Delegation——"权限与使用时间受限"。④ 可追溯——由谁签发清楚可查，日志能追到签发链。⑤ 可续期而不动长期密钥。代价：① proxy 仍是私钥，被偷后在有效期内照样能用——安全性是"把无限期风险压缩成有限窗口"，不是消灭风险。② 需要有人托管它，作业期间远端必须有可用 proxy（或刷新机制），这是新的攻击面与管理负担。③ 委托链越长越危险，若允许再签子 proxy 必须限制深度。④ 依赖本地私钥可用，续期时若密钥在硬件令牌上需要交互或特殊 agent。⑤ 短寿命不能替代审计——"有身份不等于能授权，授权后不等于不被监视"。

English answer：Its advantages are: the long-term private key never leaves the user's local machine or hardware token, so the remote node only ever holds a stand-in; the short lifetime makes the credential expire automatically at the end of the job, shrinking the exposure window to the order of hours and removing the need for someone to revoke it; the proxy can carry reduced rights and constraints, for instance access to only a few services of one VO, which is exactly the delegation requirement that rights and duration be limited; the issuer is identifiable, so usage can be traced back through the issuance chain; and it can be renewed without touching the long-term key. Its costs are: the proxy is still a private key and remains usable by a thief until it expires, so the risk is compressed into a window rather than eliminated; it has to be stored somewhere during the job, which is a new attack surface and an administrative burden; chains of delegation become hard to audit unless their depth is limited; renewal depends on local access to the long-term key, which may require interaction with a hardware token; and a short lifetime does not replace audit, because a valid identity does not imply permission, and permission does not remove the need to monitor.

(g) 过期后才被偷，为何损失有限已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：If the proxy credential is stolen only after it has expired, why is the potential damage limited? Expected: 25 words.
题干译文：若 proxy 凭据是在过期之后才被窃取，为什么潜在损失有限？（约 25 词）

笔记定位：08 章 short lifetime 与 Delegation"权限与使用时间受限"、Accounting & audit；批注"permission doesn't remove the need for audit"。

中文回答：① 验证方会检查有效期：服务端在认证时校验 validity，过期即拒绝——"偷到一张废票"不产生新的访问。② 不能续期、也不能签新凭据（在禁止 proxy 转签的配置下）：长期私钥不在远端，攻击者拿不到"生产新钥匙的模具"，无法把一次泄露延长为长期控制。③ 损害被时间盒（time-boxed）住：影响只限于过期前那段时间里、以那个受限身份做过的操作，而 Delegation 已同时限制了权限与时间。④ 可用审计界定范围：Accounting & audit 能查清"这把 proxy 在窗口内访问过什么"，便于评估与补救。

English answer：Damage is limited because the relying services check validity and reject an expired credential, so a stolen expired proxy grants no further access; because the long-term key was never on the remote machine, the attacker cannot mint fresh credentials and cannot turn a one-off theft into lasting control; and because delegation had already restricted both the rights and the lifetime, the impact is boxed into whatever operations that limited identity performed before expiry. The audit trail then establishes the scope of the damage by showing exactly what the credential accessed within that window, which is why accounting and audit are still required even when authentication succeeded.

Q11GPU 与异构计算 · GPUs and heterogeneous computing

STEMQuestion stem（原题）：A research team is simulating heat transfer through a material. The model contains 10⁷ independent cells, and during each time step the same mathematical operation is applied to every cell. The researchers are deciding whether to run the calculation on a CPU or a GPU.
题干译文：某研究团队在模拟材料中的热传导。模型包含 10⁷ 个相互独立的单元，每个时间步都对每个单元施加相同的数学运算。研究者在考虑把计算放到 CPU 还是 GPU 上运行。

(a) 为什么这个负载适合 GPU已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Explain why this workload is well suited to GPU execution. Expected: 25 words.
题干译文：解释为什么该负载适合在 GPU 上执行。（约 25 词）

笔记定位：09 章——GPU"many threads hide latency，擅长处理大量可反复利用的独立元素"；kernel"GPU 对许多元素执行的同一个函数…由 index 挑选元素，元素间无通信"；写法表 a[i] = update(a[i]) 适合。

中文回答：题目给了两条关键线索：10⁷ 个独立单元 ＋ 每个时间步对每个单元施加同一运算。① 数据并行（data parallelism）：即"同一段代码作用在不同数据上"，天然映射成"大量线程 + 同一 kernel"。② 元素间无通信：并行单元之间不共享数据、不需同步 → 没有 Tcommunication，只有 Tparallelwork/P。③ 可反复利用的独立元素：网格/时间步迭代正是"数据上了显存就在设备侧反复运算"的理想形态——一次搬运、多轮计算。④ 吞吐优先：GPU 用大量小核把 throughput 堆高、"用大量线程把延迟藏起来"，10⁷ 个单元足以喂饱它，也不属于笔记列的"不适合 GPU 的三类"（短的并行链、分支多、启动调度开销吃掉收益）。

English answer：The workload is a natural fit for a GPU because it exposes massive data parallelism: 10⁷ independent cells, each subjected to the same mathematical operation at every time step, is precisely the pattern "the same code applied to different data". The cells do not communicate, so no message passing or synchronisation is required and the cost model reduces to Tparallelwork/P with no Tcommunication term. The iteration over cells and time steps is also the ideal form of GPU work, because once the data is in device memory it is reused many times on the device — one transfer followed by many kernel launches. Finally the GPU is designed to maximise throughput by hiding latency with many threads, and 10⁷ elements are enough to keep it busy; the workload does not fall into the three unsuitable categories, namely short parallel chains, heavy branching, and launch or scheduling overhead that outweighs the gain.

(b) 反复搬运为什么会吃掉加速比已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：The CPU repeatedly copies the full dataset to the GPU, launches one kernel, copies the result back to the CPU, performs a small calculation, and then sends the data to the GPU again. Why could this design significantly reduce the expected GPU speed-up? Expected: 50 words.
题干译文：CPU 反复把完整数据集拷到 GPU、启动一个内核、把结果拷回 CPU、做一点小计算，然后再把数据送回 GPU。为什么这种设计会显著削弱预期的 GPU 加速？（约 50 词）

笔记定位：09 章——"数据搬运的开销大，故通常在 GPU 中经过多次运算后，仅把最终结果传回 CPU…把操作从 GPU 转到 CPU 会因为搬运数据效率奇差"；图 9-2"只有过这条橙色的搬运线，才产生换边的代价"。

中文回答：① 每次搬运都是 α + nβ 的钱：PCIe/NVLink 的启动延迟 + 按字节的带宽代价；10⁷ 个单元若每个是 double 加几个场变量，一次全量搬运就是数百 MB，单次搬运可能比一次 kernel 计算还贵。② 严格串行：题目模式是"拷贝→启动一个 kernel→拷贝回→CPU 小计算→再拷回"，四步无重叠，GPU 在等待时完全空闲。③ kernel 太小：每次只做一个时间步，T_kernel 很小而 T_launch + T_copy 固定 → 加速比被固定开销锁死在 1 附近，甚至比纯 CPU 更慢。④ 同步点太多：每次拷回都必须等 kernel 结束（隐式同步），无法流水。一句话公式：S = T_cpu / (T_kernel + T_transfer + T_launch + T_cpu_small)，分母里的 transfer 若与 T_cpu 同量级，则 S ≈ 1——GPU 算力再强也补偿不了"数据在路上"的时间。

English answer：The host-device round trip is the problem. Every transfer costs α + nβ, a fixed start-up latency plus a per-byte bandwidth term; with 10⁷ double-precision cells and several field variables per cell, a full copy is hundreds of megabytes and can cost more than the kernel that consumes it. The pattern described is also strictly serial — copy in, launch one kernel, copy out, do a small calculation on the CPU, copy in again — so there is no overlap and the GPU idles while data moves. Each kernel then covers only a single time step, so T_kernel is tiny while T_launch + T_copy is fixed, pinning the speedup near 1 and potentially making it slower than the CPU alone. The implicit synchronisation required before every copy back also prevents any pipelining. In short, S = T_cpu/(T_kernel + T_transfer + T_launch + T_cpu_small), and when the transfer term is comparable to the CPU time, S ≈ 1: no amount of GPU throughput compensates for time spent moving data.

(c) 更好的组织方式已收录

QUESTIONQuestion（原题）：Suggest a better strategy for organising the computation. Expected: 25 words.
题干译文：为组织该计算提出更好的策略。（约 25 词）

笔记定位：09 章——"数据搬运的开销大，故通常在 GPU 中经过多次运算后，仅把最终结果传回 CPU"；框架可代做 device-memory management / host-device data movement / kernel selection and launch。

中文回答：① 合批（batching，时间步循环下沉）：把整个时间步循环搬进设备侧——一次 H2D、跑完 N 个时间步、一次 D2H，搬运次数从 O(N) 降到 O(1)。② kernel 之间不落回 CPU：中间结果留在显存，用 device-to-device 拷贝或原地更新（double buffering a → b → a），CPU 只做"决定下一步"这类控制决策。③ 重叠与异步：用 streams 把拷贝与计算重叠、用异步拷贝（pinned memory + cudaMemcpyAsync），把搬运时间藏到计算后面。④ 减少数据量：只上送必要精度/字段（必要时单精度），只下送最终关心的结果。⑤ 工程建议：这类"内存/搬运/kernel 选择"的活优先交给 PyTorch/TensorFlow 或现成 stencil 库，"越往下设计越复杂，且回报率低"。

English answer：The dataset should stay resident on the device: copy it in once, execute the whole time-step loop as a sequence of kernels, and copy back only the final result, reducing the number of transfers from O(N) to O(1). Intermediate results must not return to the host — they stay in device memory and are updated in place or through device-to-device copies using double buffering, while the CPU only makes control decisions. Copies and computation should be overlapped with streams and asynchronous transfers from pinned memory, so that transfer time is hidden behind kernel execution. The volume of data can also be cut by transferring only the precision and fields actually needed, sending back only the quantities of interest. Finally, memory management, data movement and kernel selection are best left to a framework such as PyTorch or TensorFlow, or to a library stencil, rather than hand-written, since descending towards the hardware raises complexity while lowering the return.
